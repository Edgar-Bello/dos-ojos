FREE PUBLIC DATA, NOT A DOS OJOS FARM

2,716 thermal camera frames of a sorghum field: the TERRA-REF Field Scanalyzer at
the University of Arizona Maricopa Agricultural Center, Maricopa, Arizona,
scanned May 20, 2018 from 9:22 to 10:47 AM. Sorghum sown April 20, 2018.

LeBauer, D.S. et al. (2020). TERRA-REF, An Open Reference Data Set From High
Resolution Genomics, Phenomics, and Imaging Sensors. Dryad.
doi:10.5061/dryad.4b8gtht99. Public domain.

Each frame here is a quarter of the original width and height (1 cm a pixel
instead of 3 mm) and losslessly compressed, so the whole scan is 130 MB instead
of 3.3 GB. The temperatures and each frame's place on the ground are unchanged.
Three frames at the very end of the original download were cut off and are left out.
